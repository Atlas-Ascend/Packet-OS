# 01 — Identity and Mission

Packet OS is the atomic work substrate of VISHVARUPA. Its unit is the Packet: a typed, provenance-bearing, policy-bounded work object that survives handoffs between humans, agents, repositories, runtimes, and verification systems.

Mission: make every meaningful unit of estate work addressable, routable, observable, testable, recoverable, and provable.

Success condition: no important work exists only as chat intent, vague backlog, or invisible agent context. It becomes a packet or an explicitly linked parent/child packet set.
