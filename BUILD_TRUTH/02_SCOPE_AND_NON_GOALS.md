# 02 — Scope and Non-Goals

In scope: packet creation, typing, dependencies, priorities, permissions, lifecycle, handoffs, acceptance criteria, proof requirements, failure state, retry/escalation metadata, and closure.

Out of scope: doing the domain work itself; deciding final executive policy; independently certifying proof; replacing CrownGrid routing; replacing Workforce Spine execution; replacing Thoth/MAAT memory/state.

Packet OS coordinates work. It does not impersonate the organs that perform, authorize, verify, or remember that work.
